A high-value life operates as a four-tier stack. Lower tiers supply energy and stability to higher tiers. Higher tiers multiply time, money, and influence. If a lower tier fails, all higher tiers stop.

### 1. Domain Specifications

| Tier and Domain             | Core Parts                                                                                               | Minimum Standard                                                                                                                         | Failure Result                                                                           |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1A. Physical Health**     | Sleep, metabolism, muscle strength, heart endurance, and nervous system recovery.                        | Sleep 7 to 8 hours each night. Exercise 4 times each week. Keep body fat low.                                                            | Fatigue reduces self-control and lowers mental output.                                   |
| **1B. Emotional Control**   | Impulse control, stress tolerance, bias detection, and personal responsibility.                          | Wait 24 hours before you make emotional decisions. Separate facts from feelings. Accept full responsibility for your results.            | Emotional reactions destroy long-term plans and break trust.                             |
| **2A. High-Income Skills**  | Basic logic, fast learning, specialized skill mastery, clear writing, and persuasion.                    | Master one rare skill with high market demand. Write clear analyses. Measure the financial value of your work.                           | Common skills limit your income and remove market power.                                 |
| **2B. Operational Systems** | Deep work schedules, decision rules, task delegation, software automation, and workspace design.         | Protect 4 morning hours each day for focused work. Complete at least 15 focused hours each week. Delegate or automate routine tasks.     | Frequent interruptions waste your high-level skills.                                     |
| **3A. Capital Allocation**  | High-profit cash flow, business ownership, high-upside investments, cash reserves, and asset protection. | Save more than 30 percent of your net income. Keep 12 to 24 months of living expenses in cash. Invest extra money in high-upside assets. | Hourly wages and unprotected losses prevent financial freedom.                           |
| **3B. Strategic Network**   | Trusted peers, mutual value exchange, business alliances, and strict personal boundaries.                | Remove untrustworthy people immediately. Give value to skilled peers before you ask for help.                                            | Isolation limits opportunities, and bad contacts create legal or personal risk.          |
| **4A. Market Reputation**   | Proven work results, narrow category leadership, clear communication, and strict honesty.                | Publish proven results in one specific field. Always match your actions to your words.                                                   | Public attention without real skill exposes fraud, and dishonesty causes permanent ruin. |

### 2. System Conflicts and Defensive Rules

| Conflict or Risk            | Problem                                                                          | Action Rule                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Skill Depth vs. Network** | Deep skill requires quiet isolation, but a network requires social availability. | Block 4 morning hours for solo work. Schedule all meetings and calls in the afternoon.                         |
| **Wealth Speed vs. Safety** | Concentrated investments grow fast, but they create high financial risk.         | Hold 12 to 24 months of cash for safety. Invest only your extra money in high-risk assets.                     |
| **Public Scale vs. Time**   | High visibility attracts opportunities, but it creates constant interruptions.   | Publish articles, code, or deals instead of taking live meetings. Use automatic filters for incoming messages. |
| **Legal Protection**        | High wealth and visibility attract lawsuits.                                     | Use separate legal entities to protect your personal assets from business risks.                               |
| **Tax Protection**          | High taxes on regular wages slow down wealth growth.                             | Earn money through business ownership and long-term investments instead of regular wages.                      |

**CAUTION:** Stabilize Tier 1 and Tier 2 before you build Tier 3 or Tier 4. Always repair the lowest failing tier first.

### 3. Inspection Procedure

1. Measure your sleep hours, body fat, and resting heart rate (Domain 1A).
2. Record every emotional reaction that caused a bad decision in the last 90 days (Domain 1B).
3. **NOTE:** If Tier 1 fails the minimum standard, stop all Tier 3 and Tier 4 expansion immediately.
4. Confirm that your primary skill earns top market rates (Domain 2A).
5. Calculate your weekly hours of uninterrupted work, and cancel low-value meetings if the total is below 15 hours (Domain 2B).
6. Check that your savings rate exceeds 30 percent and your cash reserve covers 12 to 24 months (Domain 3A).
7. Remove any person from your network who breaks boundaries or drains your energy (Domain 3B).
8. Confirm that your public reputation relies on documented proof of work (Domain 4A).
9. Repair the lowest failing tier first, and then move up to the next tier.
